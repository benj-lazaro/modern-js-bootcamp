Logical OR
----------
- Logical operator wherein one (1) operand MUST be "true"
    - In order for the entire expression to be evaluated to "true"

        <operand> || <operand>

Example:

1 !== 1 || 10 === 10    // Evaluates to true
10 / 2 === 5 || null    // Evaluates to true
0 || undefined          // Evaluates to false
