Operator Precedence
-------------------
- The NOT (!) operator has the HIGHEST precedence
    - Over the AND (&&) & and OR (||) operators

    - The AND (&&) operator has a higher precedence 
        - Over the OR (||) operator

    - The OR (||) has the least precedence
        - To the NOT (!) & the AND (&&) operators

    - Precedence can be altered using a pair of ( )

    - NOTE: When in doubt of precedence hierarchy
        - Use a pair of ( ) to group operators together

    - Reference (https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/JavaScript/Reference/Operators/Operator_Precedence)
